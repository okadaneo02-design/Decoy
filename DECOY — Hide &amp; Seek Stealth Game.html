How to Play 🎭

🟦 Hider

  • Survive 4 minutes without being caught.
  • WASD move · Mouse face.
  • Q Decoy (3/round) — a fake you that walks & fools radar.
  • E Invisibility (3s)
  • Shift Dash
  • F Noise Maker — fake footsteps elsewhere.
  • Space Hide in a hiding spot (📦).

🟥 Seeker

  • Catch the Hider before time runs out.
  • Map is dark — mouse aims a flashlight cone.
  • Radar pings every 30s (direction only).
  • Shift Sprint (3s)
  • Q Tracker — reveal recent footsteps.
  • F Flash Pulse — light a big area.
  • E near target to Catch. Decoys slow you!
